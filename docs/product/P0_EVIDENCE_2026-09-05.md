## Demonstrated behavior

- A sender/customer number is not appended to the monetary amount.
- One material question is asked at a time; missing units and categories cannot
  bypass clarification. Unit conversion is visible in the reviewable preview.
- Separate objects and changes require matching confirmations. Ambiguous yes
  across active drafts is rejected; retrying an accepted confirmation is stable.
- Exact repeated messages are idempotent; duplicate content needs a choice.
- Unknown/collection/transfer/loan/other inflows never become sales; date ranges
  and excluded counts are explicit. Refunds are not silently netted into sales.
- Corrections link replacements; stale concurrent corrections cannot fork the
  same active record. A confirmed deletion removes superseded content too.
- Deleting a money record does not silently delete its linked reminder.
- Scope isolation and conservative retrieval prevent another scope's records
  appearing in searches or aggregates. Ambiguous search exposes IDs only.
- No outbound reminders, bank access, patient data or external messages exist.
